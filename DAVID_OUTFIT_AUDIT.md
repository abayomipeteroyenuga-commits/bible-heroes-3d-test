# David Outfit Audit — Biblical Hero Pass

## Status: PASS

David is locked to a biblical shepherd presentation. The active character path contains:
- Earth-tone tunic with draped front detail and subtle vertical cloth folds
- Leather belt and simple buckle
- Asymmetrical shepherd shoulder cloth
- Leather pouch at the hip
- Traditional leather sandals
- Sling attached to the right hand
- Shepherd staff attached to the left hand
- Dark wavy/curling hair, brown eyes and olive/tan skin

## Modern outfit cleanup

The previous legacy modern-David outfit implementation (jacket, T-shirt, trousers styling, sneakers and backpack) has been removed from the active source. There is no reachable modern-David outfit branch.

## Character direction

David is designed as a youthful, lean athletic biblical game hero rather than a tiny child or bulky adult. The face uses restrained eye proportions, brown irises, defined brows and a determined/courageous default expression.

## Technical note

The character remains a procedural Three.js model. It is improved toward a natural 3D game-human appearance, but it is not a photoreal scanned human asset.
