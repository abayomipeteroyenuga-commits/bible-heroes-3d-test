# Unique Worlds Update

The 10 worlds now have distinct gameplay objectives and dedicated set pieces instead of relying mainly on a shared walk-and-fight loop.

1. Shepherd's Valley — check on three sheep before clearing the valley.
2. Rocky Wilderness — discover three safe-route markers through a rock maze.
3. Forest Valley — reveal a hidden grove path before fighting and reaching the shrine.
4. Cave of Shadows — light four torches to make the cave route visible.
5. Mountain Pass — cross the bridge and climb toward the summit.
6. Philistine Outpost — secure four supply crates before reaching the gate.
7. Fortified Valley — capture four banners, then open the fortress gate.
8. Great Battlefield — secure five battlefield standards while surviving waves.
9. Goliath's Territory — inspect five giant footprints and follow the trail.
10. The Final Battle — survive the final waves, enter the arena, and face Goliath.
