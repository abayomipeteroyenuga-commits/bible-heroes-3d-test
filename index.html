📖 HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • WASD Move
  • SHIFT Run
  • SPACE Jump
  • F — FIRE SLING
  • LEFT CLICK — FIRE SLING
  • Q Faith Shield
  • E Interact / Collect
  • Mouse — Look around
  • ESC Pause

📱 Mobile

Left joystick to move. Large buttons: Jump, Attack, Faith, Interact.

❤️ Stats

Life — stay alive!
Armor — absorbs hits first
Faith — power for Faith Shield

🎯 Adventure Tips

David starts with his sling. Press F or Left Click to fire. Defeat Shadow Guardians. Reach the battlefield and defeat Goliath with courage and faith!

⚙️ SETTINGS

Controls: WASD · F / Left Click Fire Sling · Q Faith · E Interact · ESC Pause
