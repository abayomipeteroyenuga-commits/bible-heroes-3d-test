📖 HOW TO PLAY

⌨️ Desktop Controls

  • WASD / ARROW KEYS — MOVE
  • SHIFT — SPRINT (uses stamina)
  • J — JARUSCOPE SCAN
  • M — GAME MAP
  • H — EMOTES
  • F / G / T / K — FIRE SLING
  • LEFT CLICK — FIRE SLING
  • Q — FAITH SHIELD
  • C — CRAFT ARROWS
  • E — INTERACT
  • RIGHT MOUSE DRAG — LOOK AROUND
  • MOUSE WHEEL — ZOOM IN / OUT
  • ESC — PAUSE

📱 Mobile

Left joystick to move. Large buttons: Sprint, Attack, Faith, Scope, Map.

❤️ Stats

Life — stay alive!
Armor — absorbs hits first
Faith — power for Faith Shield

🎯 Adventure Tips

David starts with his sling. Press F, G, T, K, or Left Click to fire. Defeat Shadow Guardians. Finish the current world mission to unlock the next world. Use the Adventure Map to replay unlocked worlds. Stars save from your best run.

⚙️ SETTINGS

Controls: WASD · F G T K / Left Click Fire Sling · Q Faith · E Interact · ESC Pause
