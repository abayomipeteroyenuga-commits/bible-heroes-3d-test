# Character Audit — David

## PASS

David is the only playable hero and is forced through the biblical character configuration. His active silhouette is youthful, lean and athletic with slightly broad shoulders, longer legs and a proportionally reduced head.

### Face
- Olive/tan skin
- Brown eyes
- Dark wavy hair
- Defined brows and nose
- Subtle cheeks and eye highlights
- Determined/courageous default expression

### Clothing and equipment
- Earth-tone tunic with cloth drape/fold detail
- Leather belt
- Shepherd shoulder cloth
- Hip pouch
- Leather sandals
- Sling
- Shepherd staff with natural segmented/crooked profile

### Exclusions
No modern jacket, sneakers, backpack, firearm, sci-fi armor or superhero costume is part of the active David path.

### Limitation
The model is procedural Three.js geometry and standard materials. It is intentionally naturalistic for the game but should not be described as true photorealistic human geometry.
